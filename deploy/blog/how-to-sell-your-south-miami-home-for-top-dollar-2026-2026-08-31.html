Selling a home in South Miami is about more than putting a sign in the yard. The sellers who win in South Miami follow a deliberate playbook. Here it is.

The first two weeks on market generate the most attention. Price your South Miami home accurately and you create competition; overprice it and you lose your best buyers.

South Miami buyers expect a polished, move-in-ready presentation. Strategic prep, decluttering, and staging routinely return several times their cost.

3. Market aggressively

Professional photography, video, targeted digital advertising, and broad exposure are non-negotiable in South Miami's competitive market for townhomes & condos.

A skilled local agent protects your net proceeds through inspection, appraisal, and closing — where deals are often won or lost.
